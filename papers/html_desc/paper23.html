Papers XV and XVIII fix the QH=3 baryon as the (E6)1 trefoil T2,3 and show its formation is a satellite-threading reconnection that topologically forces a QH=1 pion byproduct. Paper XXIII builds that process at the field level and reads off its framing and density-feedback energetics. The organizing fact: the trefoil is the optimal QH=3 shape but is never realized in isolation — it exists only through pion emission/absorption — so static operations give the optimal-shape invariants while the dynamical questions (existence, the saddle, the formation energy) live on the reaction coordinate.

  • A charge-correct, singularity-free knotted map The Brieskorn doublet u = (a2+c b3)/a on S3 realises the trefoil Hopfion with the Whitehead charge QH = 3 (Richardson-extrapolated Q∞ = 3.03) and matches the physical thin-tube virial Vmax = 1.871 vs 1.875 to <0.3%. Its u=0 preimage is genuinely knotted (off-axis, two-fold toroidal winding), where the earlier tube-lift (QH ≈ 0) and one-dimensional torus reduction both fail.
  • The pion as an explicit field process A single-valued reaction coordinate interpolates the QH=2 Hopf link to the QH=3 trefoil; the net Hopf charge changes by +1 across a reconnection barrier at λ* ≈ 0.35–0.40, realising the Paper XVIII pion not as a bookkeeping byproduct but as a field-level change of topological charge. The two cable strands of the single knotted circle (inner/outer passes at 2r0) fuse across the barrier — the formation reconnection.
  • The pion is a twist quantum By Calugareanu–White the Hopf charge of a framed curve is its self-linking Wr + Tw. The emitted QH=1 pion is the unknot: a round unknot has Wr = 0, so its unit of charge is pure twist. The trefoil carries its charge as writhe; the barrier is a twist↔writhe reconfiguration, and the pion carries one twist quantum on emission.
  • Torsion stays perturbative at the barrier Paper XV's exact torsion cancellation holds only for the symmetric profile; off it, the surviving correction is curvature-gated (∝ ⟨κ2τ2⟩). Tracked along the non-symmetric reaction coordinate — with the numerical Frenet machinery gated against Paper XV's ⟨κ2⟩=0.120, ⟨κ2τ2⟩=0.0053 — the torsion correction stays ≤ a few percent across the barrier and does not spike at the reconnection.
  • The relaxed trefoil is self-avoiding The minimal non-adjacent strand distance is the constant 2r0 = 1.748, well outside the field-tube width 2/C3* = 0.80, so the field tubes do not overlap. The density-feedback saturation is therefore a single-tube wall quantity, not an inter-strand overlap; Paper XV's ‘self-interaction at the crossings’ is the curvature peaking there, not a tube overlap. Overlap occurs only transiently, during the threading of the barrier.
  • Intrinsic feedback floor versus the environment value The trefoil's intrinsic self-consistent feedback argument is scale- and medium-independent, β*κ = 8b* ≈ 0.54 (the Battye–Sutcliffe core kernel is bounded; adding an ambient background does not raise it). This is not the β*ρ ∼ 15 that sets the baryon asymmetry: that is the environment (chameleon) feedback of the dense QCD-epoch condensate, bracketed by the first-order window and freeze-out-selected at Tc (Paper XXII). The intrinsic floor is a subdominant addition; the two must not be conflated.
  • Existence as a pion-emission transition state Because the feedback energy Efb = Kfb J4 is a product, the QH=3 object is a saddle, not a minimum (Paper XX), so gradient flow — which seeks minima — cannot converge to it, explaining the long-standing non-convergence. Along the reaction coordinate Efb(λ) shows an interior barrier at λ* = 0.40 (converged over N=128–192) with QH passing 2→3 (the pion): an index-1 saddle by the mountain-pass argument. The QH=3 configuration therefore exists as the index-1 transition state of its own formation, supplying the charge-conserving construction Paper XXI (open-problem (ii)) identifies as required.

A scale–coupling degeneracy underlies the method: under dilation the virial ratio obeys V(λ,β) = V(β/λ2), so a dilation is equivalent to rescaling the feedback coupling — the density-feedback form of Paper I's Derrick/dilation instability. A stationary-point search over scale and coupling together is therefore degenerate (it certifies any shape); the physical coupling is held fixed and the existence statement rests on the reaction coordinate. What remains open is the transition-state energy in physical units (which fixes the first-order window location of Paper XXII): since the trefoil is transient — no isolated minimum to relax to — this is a formation-energy evaluation gated by the tube-resolution wall, not a static saddle-finding step. The analogous charge-conserving construction for the beyond–Standard-Model D-type dark sector of Paper XXI remains to be carried out.
