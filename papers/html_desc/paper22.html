Paper XXI identifies the beyond–Standard-Model D-type sector as a sterile, Dirac dark-matter candidate. Paper XXII is its dynamical companion: how baryons and the dark sector are produced together in the early universe. It derives the confinement transition as first-order from the condensate's own thermal free energy, maps the three Sakharov conditions onto knot dynamics, and shows that because the CP phase belongs to the shared 2I vacuum, one event seeds correlated baryon and dark asymmetries (co-genesis). All three Sakharov conditions are derived; the single quantity that remains open is the magnitude of the baryon asymmetry, which reduces to one density-feedback number.

  • The confinement transition is first-order, derived from the epoch's thermal free energy The trefoil's three colours supply the ℤ3 cubic of a Polyakov-loop Landau–Ginzburg potential, present by construction (the colour-singlet baryon is the ℤ3 invariant 3×3×3→1). The explicit breaking is small — h ∼ 0.02–0.14, below the critical endpoint hc ≈ 0.21 — because the framework, unlike real QCD, has no free light quarks: ℤ3 is broken only by string-breaking. The full free energy F(T,L) has its minimum jump discontinuously from the confined branch to L→1 across a finite barrier (ΔL ≈ 0.55–0.65), with a modest latent heat α ≈ 0.03–0.06: strongly first-order in order-parameter space.
  • The potential's coefficients come from the geometry of the epoch The Landau–Ginzburg coefficients are not free: they are the holonomy expansion of the thermal free energy of condensate excitations carrying the medium's semi-Dirac dispersion (linear along the easy radial axis, quadratic along the hard perpendicular axis). The anisotropic phase space changes the temperature power, ∫d3k e−nE/T ∝ (T/n)2, giving a cubic-Bernoulli (B3) Weiss potential rather than the relativistic quartic; summing the three SU(3) colour holonomy phases enhances the cubic (b3/b4 ≈ 0.17), strengthening the first-order character, and the gap's thermal mass changes sign at Tc ∼ 2Λcond, matching the framework's near-critical condition T/Λcond ≈ 2.
  • Baryogenesis as chiral reconnection, with automatic shutoff The three Sakharov conditions map onto knot dynamics: baryon-number violation is the sector-changing reconnection QH=2+QH=2→QH=3+QH=1 (two leptons threading into a baryon plus a neutrino byproduct); C and CP violation are the derived Wess–Zumino–Witten phase δCP=65.0° (sin δCP=0.91, of order unity) together with the chiral trefoil writhe; and the departure from equilibrium is the first-order transition. The shutoff is structural rather than conditional: a reconnection needs free trefoil strands, which exist only in the deconfined phase, so once a confined bubble forms the baryon-number-violating rate stops automatically — confinement itself plays the role of the electroweak ‘sphaleron shutoff’.
  • Co-genesis and the asymmetric-dark-matter ratio Because δCP belongs to the shared 2I vacuum, which contains both 2T (baryon) and 2D3 (dark) as subgroups, the same reconnection biases both freeze-outs, seeding correlated asymmetries; the Dirac D-type sector carries a dark number, its symmetric part self-annihilating through the shared director field. The number-asymmetry ratio r=ndark/nb ∼ O(1) is independent of the unpinned η (the common transport efficiency cancels), so ΩDM/Ωb=r (mdark/mp) ≈ 5 fixes mdark ≈ 5 GeV — a conditional consistency resting on r ∼ O(1), efficient self-annihilation, and the relic's existence (Paper XXI), not a parameter-free prediction.
  • Two-epoch dark matter: one fabric, two modes, resolved by geometry The D-type relic and the baryon-sourced director strain of Paper VII dominate at two epochs. The relic — non-baryonic, sterile, non-ultralight (m ≫ Hrec), and adiabatic through co-genesis — is the cold dark matter the acoustic peaks and cluster lensing require, where the late, isocurvature strain alone falls short. At z ∼ 0 the strain yields the galactic radial-acceleration relation by tracking baryons, and the relic does not spoil it because its self-interaction inherits the Seff ∝ sin4θ/φ6 anisotropy of the shared director field — the same kernel whose geometry-dependence drives the Bell correlations of Papers VIII–IX. The interaction is resolved and active where the field is coherent (inside galaxies: it relaxes the relic, which tracks baryons) and washes out where the field is isotropic (recombination and inter-galactic space: collisionless, no over-closure) — the same geometry-gating the Bell sector exhibits, a shared falsifiable signature (planar vs spherical).

The one quantity not derived is the magnitude of η: the CP interference and the departure from equilibrium are both O(1), so efficient reconnection overproduces, and the required suppression is carried by the heavy-band anisotropy Seff ∝ sin4θ/φ6 of the baryon channel (the φ6 a physical WZW/Bogomolny power, ≈0.056 per power). The fixed, physical φ6p does most of the work: for the pinned power p=2 the φ12 ≈ 322 supplies all but a residual factor ∼2.5×102, which is the feedback term (1+β*ρ)2 with β*ρ ∼ 15. The power is pinned, not assumed: the reconnection 2+2→3+1 has exactly one heavy-band object (the QH=3 baryon), entering the amplitude once, so the rate ∝ |amplitude|2 gives p=2. The feedback β*ρ is not a free knob either: baryogenesis is trefoil formation, so it is the baryon sector's own deeply-saturated feedback (the same saturation that denies the quark a static saddle), and the established trefoil sharpness C3*=2.5062 places it in the saturated range by order of magnitude. The single remaining open quantity is the precise value of β*ρ — whether the self-consistent trefoil core in fact reaches ∼15 — which requires a charge-resolving solve of the sharp trefoil and is order-of-magnitude plausible but not yet established. The co-genesis ratio ΩDM/Ωb ∼ O(5) is independent of all of this and stands as the sector's falsifiable prediction.
