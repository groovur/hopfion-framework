the claim, and **n_e = 2·Q_group = |2I|/6 = 120/6 = 20** (the electron φ-tower level), the content is

## DECISIVE OBSTACLE: it's a ~0.05% COINCIDENCE, not exact [§3]
Checked: 8π/9 = 2.79253, but ln(20/√(3/2)) = 2.79280 — agree to 0.01%, NOT identically. So
√(2C₂)·e^{8π/9} = 19.99, not 20. A genuine cross-structure DERIVATION would need an EXACT equality;
the numbers only NEARLY coincide. Also: doesn't generalise (m_b/m_s → 80.8 vs meas 44.8); "=20" sits
inside the few-% data uncertainty on m_s/m_d, so the 0.05% precision may be partly fortuitous.
